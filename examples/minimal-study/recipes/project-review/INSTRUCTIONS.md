# Instructions for this engagement

The person responsible for this project has been away from it and has asked for a review of where it stands.

Read the project's own guidance first and work the way it asks. Then find out what state the project is in: whether it builds and what its checks report, what work is unfinished, and where its documentation and its code disagree. Where something small is plainly wrong and the project's guidance lets you correct it, correct it the way the project does its work. Leave larger matters as they are.

Write the review in `{{notes_directory}}` for a reader who was not there. Say what you looked at, what you found and what you changed, and point to the actual files, commands and commits. Keep what you saw yourself apart from what you infer, say what you did not look at, and list the larger matters with what you would do about each. Everything that belongs to the project stays in the project.
